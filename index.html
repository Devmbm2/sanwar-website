FAQ

Questions we hear on every first call.

Which CRM should I use?

It depends on your team size, budget and how you sell. We compare options such as HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive and GoHighLevel against your process on the discovery call and recommend one, with no reseller bias.

Can you move my data from spreadsheets or my old CRM?

Yes. We clean, de-duplicate and map your contacts, companies, deals and history, then migrate and verify everything before you switch over.

How long does a project take?

A standard CRM setup takes two to three weeks. Larger automation projects take four to six weeks, and custom CRM builds are scoped individually.

Do I need a custom CRM or an off-the-shelf one?

Most businesses are best served by an off-the-shelf CRM with good automation. We recommend custom development only when your workflow genuinely doesn't fit, or when licence costs at your team size make a custom build cheaper.

What happens after launch?

You get training, written documentation and a support period. Many clients keep a monthly support plan so we can add automations and adjust the CRM as they grow.
